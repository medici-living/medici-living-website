Customised to the business

One AI foundation.
Different workflows.

The goal is not a generic chatbot or a fixed package. It is an adaptable operating layer configured to each business’s needs, using approved data and connected tools to help automate work, verify outcomes and report what happened.

01 / Command centre

A clearer view of the day

Bring priorities, outstanding tasks, leads, quotes and project activity into one place, with natural-language questions grounded in accessible records.

02 / Sales & CRM

Keep opportunities moving

Organise prospects, capture customer needs, identify overdue follow-ups and prepare relevant next steps without losing conversation history.

03 / Operations

From enquiry to delivery

Support scope-of-work drafts, quote preparation, job checklists, cost tracking and project updates — with checks and approval before consequential commitments.

04 / Marketing

Consistent, measured content

Plan campaigns, draft platform-specific content, organise approvals and track whether publishing actually succeeded.

05 / Connected tools

Automation you can trust

Coordinate approved workflows across data, files and external services, with permissions, logs, safe retries and duplicate protection.

06 / Business intelligence

Decisions with context

Help analyse sales, operations, marketing and financial performance using available data — clearly separating actual results from estimates and forecasts.
